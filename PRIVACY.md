# Privacy Policy — EvenLyrics Companion

- **What it reads:** title, artist, duration and playback position of the media session currently playing (via Android Notification Access). It does not read notification text from other apps for any other purpose.
- **What it sends:** only the song title, artist and duration to the public LRCLIB API (https://lrclib.net) to find synced lyrics.
- **Local only:** playback state and lyrics are passed to the EvenLyrics plugin over `127.0.0.1:5288` on the same phone.
- **Not collected:** no microphone or audio, no account, no analytics, no advertising IDs, no data stored on our servers (there are none).
- **Contact:** litas.techlab@gmail.com

# 隱私政策（中文）
本 App 僅讀取正在播放歌曲的歌名、歌手、長度與進度；僅將歌名、歌手與長度傳給 LRCLIB 查詢歌詞；資料只在手機本機傳給 EvenLyrics 插件。不使用麥克風、不錄音、不收集帳號或分析資料。聯絡：litas.techlab@gmail.com
